The Harbor analytics migration begins on September 1.
Dana Choi is responsible for the rollout.
The first phase moves billing dashboards, weekly revenue reports, and anomaly alerts to the new warehouse.
Access is limited to the finance and operations teams during the first month.
The team will validate row-level parity every day and track query latency, refresh failures, and stakeholder-reported data issues.
If parity remains above 99.5% for two weeks, the legacy pipeline will be turned off on September 30.
